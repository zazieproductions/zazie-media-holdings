RADIOREACH FUNNEL • PROPRIETARY BEHAVIORAL IP

Marketing Psychology & Conversion Systems

Repository-described mechanics for the Emotional State Proxy and Momentum + Ownership sequence.

EXPAND SYSTEM DETAILS ↓

RadioReach Funnel is described as a conversion-first commerce experience. Beyond checkout and delivery, its README documents behavioral, psychological, and gamification systems intended to guide a visitor from discovery toward purchase. The README presents these systems as feature-flagged, measurable, tested, and designed to degrade gracefully when disabled; it says the underlying offer, price, product facts, and terms do not change. The mechanics below are summarized from repository documentation and have not been independently audited.

01 — ADAPTIVE COPY SYSTEM
Emotional State Proxy (ESP)

ESP is a client-side behavioral state engine that interprets how a visitor reads the sales page and adapts the tone of the copy—not the price, product facts, or offer terms. The README characterizes the adaptation as subtle and says many visitors may not notice the mechanism; this register explicitly describes how it works.

HOW IT WORKS

Passive, non-blocking listeners for scroll velocity, direction changes, rage-click signals, deliberate reading patterns, CTA hover, key-section revisits, and idle-debate detection feed a normalized signal layer. A weighted decision process applies exponential decay (20-second half-life). According to the README, a mode change must clear a minimum score, lead the runner-up by a margin, hold for 500 ms, and respect an 8-second cooldown. Noisy or contradictory signals fall back to default copy.

THE THREE MODES
MODEDOCUMENTED SIGNALSCOPY TONE
calmFast/aggressive scrolling, direction flips, rage-click signals, or long idle.Soften, simplify, and de-pressure (for example, “No BS, take your time”).
aspireSlow, deliberate reading and pauses around proof sections.Identity and future-oriented framing for a serious artist.
urgencyRepeated returns to pricing, scarcity, or CTAs; lingering on CTAs or idling at price.A “cost of waiting” frame; the README says it should not change offer terms.
WHY IT MATTERS

The repository frames ESP as responding differently to fast, frustrated browsing and slower engagement with proof, while keeping the same underlying offer. It is intended to help the page meet a visitor’s current decision state and measure which mode correlates with conversion.

MEASUREMENT

A shared CTA handler records a conversion event with the current mode, scores, signal counts, and session ID; the success page records purchase_completed with the last persisted mode. The README identifies sessionStorage["esp.events.v1"] as the local event log.

SAFETY CONTROLS

The repository documents four ways to disable ESP: URL parameter, localStorage, build-time environment flag, or source removal. It says that when disabled, observers, listeners, and logging stop and the original copy renders. See the ESP implementation notes ↗.

TRANSPARENCY & PRIVACY REVIEW

Scroll, click, and intent signals can be personal data. Before deployment, verify disclosures, consent and opt-out behavior, data minimization, retention, and applicable privacy requirements. Avoid inferring sensitive traits. This listing is not a privacy or compliance audit.

02 — FEATURE-FLAGGED INTENT SEQUENCE
Momentum + Ownership Sequence

A feature-flagged, three-step interactive sequence that invites readers to articulate a target market, a key frustration, and what they are ready to leave behind before checkout.

DESIGN & PSYCHOLOGY

The README frames the sequence around the endowment effect and consistency principle: the visitor’s responses are reflected back as a personalized starting plan. Three quick prompts—target, frustration, and “done-with”—receive acknowledgements; a progress bar uses stage language such as “Preparing your access,” “Shaping your contact plan,” and “Organizing your starting move”; answers persist in localStorage so returning visitors can resume; and the final screen presents a named outcome such as “YOUR COLLEGE RADIO PLAN IS SET.”

MEASUREMENT

The documented analytics lifecycle includes MomentumSequenceViewed, Started, StepCompleted, Finished, CtaClicked, and Reset. Normalized sequence_* attribution is attached to checkout and purchase events to compare sequence completion with conversion and order value. The feature flag, persistence behavior, and tracking should be verified against the deployed build and clearly disclosed to visitors.
