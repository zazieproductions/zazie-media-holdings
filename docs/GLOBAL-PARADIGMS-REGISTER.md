# Global Paradigms Corp — archive register

`globalparadigmscorp.com` is the second property in the domains portfolio and
the only one with a two-act history: a 2006 corporate-conspiracy hoax that
went dark, and a 2026 revival that reuses the name without continuing the
story. The domain card in `#domains` now opens an **archive register** instead
of a three-stat summary, and every line in it is transcribed from a named
source.

## What the card shows

`GPC_REGISTER` in `src/app.js` holds the register; `renderDomainRegister()`
renders it inside the domain modal, and `DOMAIN_REGISTERS` maps a domain's
`register:"gpc"` key to it. Eight types of information are published:

| Type | Content |
|---|---|
| Canon status | Lostpedia's non-canon hoax alert, and the 2026 project's lack of affiliation with ABC, Disney, the creators of *Lost*, or the original GPC creators |
| Identity sheet | 12 rows — domain, original era, Wayback record, 2006 site map, copyright line, the fictional Chapter 11 filing, jurisdiction, reactivation, the live 2026 build, the 2026 continuity, the current ARG, canon status |
| Timeline | 12 dated entries, 1993-04-08 → 2026-10-18 (employment record, bankruptcy notice, first capture, the 2006 Lostpedia debate, the capture of the client list, the shut-down, the revival note, today's read) |
| Roster | Seven people and organisations — Enrico Valenzetti, Michael Sontag, S.K. Nave, RansomX Ministries, Grendel, Gil Flores, Project Chthonic |
| Client roster | The twelve names published on the archived 2006 Clients page |
| Transmissions | The three hidden messages: the Stephenson addendum, the Morse code, Grendel's UPC barcode |
| Primary-source quote | Terms of Use §8 "Sensitive Material", transcribed as published |
| Primary sources | 10 outbound links — the Lostpedia article, talk page and hoax category, six archived 2006 pages, and the live 2026 site |

## What is sourced, and what is still demo

Sourced (transcribed, and checkable at the links above): the 2006 copyright
line, the Chapter 11 notice, the client roster, the Terms of Use §8 wording,
the jurisdiction, the personnel, the hidden messages, the capture dates, the
non-canon status, and the figures the 2026 build publishes about itself.

Still illustrative demo metrics on the card — unchanged, unverified, and
flagged in the content audit: `140K /yr` traffic, `$9K ads + lore drops`
revenue, `100%` ownership, and the "400+ pages" claim in the card copy. The
live site publishes **174** indexed records; the card's "400+" figure is a
demo number and should be replaced with the site's own count before the page
is published as a business record.

## Publication gates

1. **The 2006 site was an intentional hoax.** Lostpedia files it under
   Category:Hoax: fake material built to be mistaken for official *Lost*
   sources — more confusing than fanon (labelled) or parody (humorous). The
   modal opens with that notice; keep it wherever the register is shown.
2. **Nothing in the register is a company record.** The 1995 bankruptcy, the
   client roster, the personnel and the "reactivation" are fiction or a
   separate ARG. Do not present any of it as a filing, a contract, a client
   list, an employment record or a corporate statement.
3. **The 2026 project is not *Lost* canon and is not affiliated with ABC,
   Disney or the creators of the show.** It is an independent ARG and
   experimental web project that borrows the name and the mood of the 2006
   site. It has its own continuity — Paradigms Systems Ltd., Cambridge,
   1971 — which is not a continuation of the 1995 story.
4. **The 2026 build is a separate work.** Do not describe it as a restoration
   or a revival of the original site; Lostpedia is explicit that it is a new
   project using the history and identity of the domain.
5. **Lostpedia's own text is CC BY-NC-ND.** The register quotes short
   excerpts with attribution and links back; do not republish the wiki's
   article wholesale or adapt it.
6. **Re-verify before publishing.** The live-site counts, the capture count
   and the 2026 ARG state change as the project runs. The 2006 archive does
   not change; the 2026 material does.
