Synopsis, as published on the FilmFreeway project page:

> There's no plot, no characters, no resolution—only a quiet, relentless immersion into the shimmer between perception and collapse. Beyond The Silken Threads is a contemporary gesture toward psychedelic cinema, but instead of the ecstatic or the indulgent, it offers something slower, more feral: a crawling, mournful beauty that leaves you altered.
>
> Insects dominate the screen—macro shots of ants locked in ritual, winged predators twitching on leaf-litter, larvae spasming in shallow focus. These aren't metaphors. They're events, textures, signs. And they repeat, distort, overlay, blur. A bridge hangs in the air like a hallucinated ruin. A figure walks, dissolves, returns. Everything is too sharp or too soft. Colors lean toward hyperreal: bruise-violet, overripe orange, chlorophyll green. The sky stutters.
>
> The editing is feverish but never frantic—images bleed into one another, like memory misfiring under emotional duress. The soundtrack hums with low drones, reverberant clatter, what might be the inside of a throat or the slow scan of a dying cassette. The film is unafraid of silence, of waiting.
>
> At its core is the disquiet of derealization—where the world feels proximate but unreachable, rendered in unsettling detail. Each frame pulses with a sense of being both too late and too early, like déjà vu built from nature documentaries, dream fragments, and corrupted home videos.
>
> This isn't dream logic—it's the logic of something just waking up, not quite sure it wants to.

The site quotes the published text in full in the card modal, paragraph breaks included.

Roles (Stage 32): Composer, Director, Producer, Sound Designer, Editor, Cinematographer, Visual Effects Artist.

Awards and selections: Special Jury Mention, *Re:Fract | Festival for Nonlinear Futures*, Ljubljana, 13 March 2025 (Eastern European premiere).

## 4 — Sepsis

Not a FilmFreeway project. This is the work shown in the group exhibition *ripples* at [New Media Art Space](https://newmediartspace.info/exhibitions/2024_ripples/sepsis.html) in 2024, and published with the exhibition's own project description:

| Field | Value (exhibition page) |
|---|---|
| Form | Audiovisual piece |
| Runtime | 2 minutes 51 seconds (as printed in the embedded player) |
| Exhibited | *ripples*, New Media Art Space, 2024 |
| Artist | Zazie Productions |
| Method | Hyper-edited montage; the curatorial statement notes the drawing on Stan Brakhage's maximalist style |

Project description, as published:

> Sepsis (2024) is an audiovisual piece that captures the overstimulating nature of media through a relentless visual and auditory barrage. Just as the body's immune response to infection can go into septic shock, so too can our cognitive defenses turn against us. Screens multiply across various media—trucks, TVs, computers—creating an infinite visual loop that mirrors the non-stop flow of information we navigate daily. Human faces distort progressively in these sequences, symbolizing our gradual detachment from physical reality as digital personas take precedence. The atmosphere is intensified by "personalized" meditative, disembodied voices, enhancing the alienation. Drawing on Stan Brakhage's maximalist style, the piece layers dense, provocative imagery to confront viewers with the sensory overload characteristic of modern existence.

The exhibition's curatorial statement adds: *Sepsis* "commentates on the disconnection running rampant in the current digital age… a display of small waves spreading and warping to the extent of visual mayhem, creating a perpetual cycle where our representation of the world corrupts our surroundings."

### Refreshing or replacing a cover

- Drop a replacement at `public/art/films/<slug>.jpg` — 2:3 portrait, 800–1200 px per side, sRGB, JPEG quality ~82, under ~200 KB. The local file always wins.
- If a local file is removed, the card falls back to the upstream still on its own; no code change is needed.
- The FilmFreeway stills are served from FilmFreeway's public press-kit CDN. If those URLs are replaced, update the `remote` field for that film in `src/app.js`.
- *Sepsis* has no local file yet. The exhibition still is hotlinked because the exhibition page publishes it; copying it locally is a one-file job whenever the artist wants the section free of third-party image requests.
