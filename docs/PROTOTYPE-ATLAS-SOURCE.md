# Prototype Atlas source and chart notes

The local `public/data/prototypes.json` snapshot is derived from the 160 records in
[`src/data/collections/prototypes.json`](https://github.com/zazieproductions/ZAZIE-INSTITUTE-OF-APPLIED-ANOMALIES/blob/c4fa36a4a2292f3f843cb25d166e8e9ba85610f7/src/data/collections/prototypes.json)
of the Zazie Institute of Applied Anomalies public archive (commit
`c4fa36a4a2292f3f843cb25d166e8e9ba85610f7`). Source: Zazie Institute of
Applied Anomalies / Zazie Productions LLC, *ZIAA Research Archive*, version
2026.09. The snapshot retains the source's technical summaries, specifications,
interface protocols, BOMs, calibration profiles, relationships, deployments and
safety notes. URLs point to the corresponding original public dossiers.

The atlas's operating-band chart maps published endpoints onto a logarithmic
axis. Its schematic illustrations indicate the source record's schematic **type**;
they do not reproduce the Institute's original technical drawings or measured
frequency response. Open a source dossier for its original vector schematic and
interactive acoustic bench. Calibration numbers are source data, not live signals.
