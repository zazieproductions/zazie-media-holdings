## Publication gates

1. **Acceptance is not publication.** Both accepted pieces are forthcoming; confirm publication dates and final titles with the editors before presenting them as published credits. The Orchard of Eyelids was accepted under an email subject that spelled the title "Orchid"; the body and manuscript say "Orchard" — confirm which the anthology will print.
2. **Statuses expire.** The tracker is dated 2026-10-09. Re-run it before launch; three submissions still have no recorded outcome.
3. **Do not publish personal contact details.** `Signage_Parallax_Submission.docx` opens with a phone number and email address. Neither appears on the site, and neither should.
4. **Rights and consent.** Confirm the artist controls every shown artwork and that the co-branded ZAZIE PRODUCTIONS × FLESHWEATHER wordmark has the other party's consent before commercial use. The archive note says the mark is a lockup, not a solo mark.
5. **Excerpt scope.** The site quotes openings and selected stanzas, not complete texts, so the manuscripts remain submission-eligible. Keep it that way unless a piece is published or formally withdrawn from circulation.
6. **Submission strategy.** Several pieces are labelled READY or UNSENT and may still be under consideration elsewhere. Check simultaneous-submission policies before treating an excerpt as free to republish.
